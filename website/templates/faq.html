Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Orbitachat's security, privacy, and technology.

How does Orbitachat's End-to-End Encryption work?

Orbitachat uses the industry-standard Double Ratchet algorithm, combined with X25519 for key agreement. Every single message gets a unique, ephemeral encryption key. Even if your current key is compromised, past messages remain secure (Perfect Forward Secrecy), and future messages quickly heal the key stream (Future Secrecy).

Do you collect any metadata?

No. Orbitachat operates on a strict zero-metadata philosophy. We do not require phone numbers or email addresses. We do not store who you talk to, when you talk to them, or your IP addresses. Our servers are designed to route encrypted blobs from point A to point B and forget them immediately after delivery.

Is Orbitachat open source?

Yes, 100%. Our client applications and server infrastructure are fully open source. We believe that true security requires transparency. Anyone can audit our codebase, verify our cryptographic implementations, and compile the applications themselves.

Can I build the application from source?

Absolutely. You can clone our GitHub repository and build the desktop client or mobile apps using standard build tools. Detailed instructions are provided in the README files of our respective repositories.
